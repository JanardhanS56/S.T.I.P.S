# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do **not** open a public issue. Contact: `[OWNER/OFFICE SECURITY CONTACT]`. Include what you found, steps to reproduce, and impact. We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours and will keep you informed. Please avoid accessing other users' data, degrading the service, or testing on production accounts you do not own.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release on `main` receives fixes.

## Scope

The S.T.I.P.S web app and API. Out of scope: third-party services (Vercel, Supabase, Upstash) except misconfiguration on our side.
