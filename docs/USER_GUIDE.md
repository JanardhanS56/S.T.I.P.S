# Guide for students and teachers

**Signing in.** Your office gives you a username and a first password. Sign in, then choose your own password (at least 10 characters). You are placed in your institute's community automatically, or you enter the code the office gave you.

**Community feed.** Share a notice, an event, a meme or a funny moment in up to 280 characters. Filter by category. Use Report on anything that is not okay.

**Polls & votes.** Anyone can start a poll (2 to 6 options, open for 1 to 7 days). You get one vote per poll and cannot change it. Results show once you vote or the poll closes.

**Messages.** Message a teacher or the office by username. Students cannot message other students. Phone numbers and email addresses cannot be sent.

**Profile.** Change your display name, bio and password. Others in your community see only your name, username, role and bio.

**Be kind.** No bullying, hate, adult content or sharing personal details. The office can mute or suspend accounts that break the guidelines.
