# Guide for the principal / office

## Your role
You run your institute's community. You decide who is in it and you keep it friendly.

## First time
1. The platform operator gives you a community code, a username and a one-time password.
2. Sign in and choose your own password.

## Adding teachers and students
Create > Admin tools > Add members. Type one person per line: `Name, teacher` or `Name, student`. Press Create usernames. A table appears with each person's username and one-time password. **Copy it now**: passwords are never shown again. Hand them out in person or through your normal official channel. People set their own password at first sign-in.

People who were not added can join with your community code only if they already hold an account without a community.

## Keeping it friendly
- Open reports appear under Admin tools. Hide the content or dismiss the report.
- A person can be muted (can read, cannot post, vote or message) or suspended (cannot sign in).
- Forgotten password? Reset it for the person; you will get a new one-time password.

## Things to decide with your management
Who may use it (age rules, consent), how long data is kept, and who can be reached when a serious concern is reported. The privacy text in the app is a starting point, not legal advice.
